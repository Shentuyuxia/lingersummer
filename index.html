  • Linden: The Hippie Heart of Hannover22 Aug 2025, CN

    Linden is not Hannover’s most glamorous district. Here, walls are awash with graffiti, kiosks line the streets, and music and conversation spill into the open. Young people, immigrants, artists and descendants of working-class families live side by side, making it the city’s freest and least easily defined neighbourhood.
